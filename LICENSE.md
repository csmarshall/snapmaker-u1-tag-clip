## What that means in practice

**You may**, free of charge: print the parts for yourself, your household, your workplace or a makerspace; change the designs and share your versions, as long as you credit the original authors and share under the same licence; use the tooling in `tools/` for anything, including commercial work.

**You may not**, without permission: sell these files, prints made from them, or remixes of them. That includes Etsy, eBay, Amazon and print-on-demand services, credited or not. If you'd like to sell prints, open an issue and ask.
