Round 2: fit set

    Sticker pause: chip only

    The chip seals its NTAG215 sticker inside, so it is the one print that needs a pause. The sticker sits on a 0.6 mm back skin in a pocket that is open until the layer starting at 1.20 mm.

    1. In the slicer, add a pause at the layer starting at 1.20 mm (Orca: right-click the layer slider, then Add pause).
    2. When the printer pauses, drop the sticker in, chip side up, and press it flat.
    3. Resume. The next layers bridge over and seal it.

    Drag the height slider, or press "Stop at the chip's pause", to see the open pocket. The sticker's 0.4 mm thickness is assumed, not measured. The fixtures need no pause: their stickers drop in after printing.
