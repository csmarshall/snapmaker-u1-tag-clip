How to print it

Chip
Back face down. Pause at the layer that starts at 1.20 mm, drop the sticker in, resume. The chip is 2.4 mm thick, with 0.6 mm of plastic under the sticker.
Dock
Standing on its bottom stop (flat end down), so the cover, lips and funnel are all vertical or slope upward.
Two colours
Body plus label STL for each chip, and dock plus digit STL for each slot, sharing one origin.

Review findings and what changed

Open

Mount and read range still unknown

The dock doesn't attach to the U1 yet, and nobody has measured how much plastic the reader sees through. Sealing the sticker inside the chip adds 0.6 mm, so the sticker now sits behind 1.8 mm of plastic plus a 0.3 mm gap.

Next: print the standoff strip, the coin-finder puck, one chip and one dock. If the reader is marginal, the back plate (DOCK_FLOOR) or the skin under the sticker (BACK_SKIN) gets thinner.

Fixed

The top rim is one flat plane

The notches came from separate wedge cuts at different depths. The mouth is now one ruled loft from the chip's path up to a flared opening, cut from every part at once, so the back plate, channels and lips all end on the same flat rim with an even bevel inside. A test checks that every point near the top sits at one height.

Fixed

Printable without bridges

The sticker sits in a sealed pocket filled at a pause, so its ceiling prints on top of the sticker. The dock prints upright. The lips have 45° lower ends, and the lower pocket is closed at the bottom by a full-depth bottom stop.

Fixed

Upside-down chips can't seat

The flat end is 6 mm longer with a centre notch, and a 5 mm key post stands on the bottom stop. Put in round end first, the chip lands on the post and sits about 5 mm high; a test sweeps a flipped chip down to confirm it.

Fixed

One clearance, named parts, checks in the repo

Every sliding gap derives from FIT_CLEARANCE (0.3 mm; 0.6 mm over the clip arms). The dock is built as back plate, channels and lower pocket, and these are exported as separate files, which this viewer loads directly. The side-slot code is gone, since the clip is empty when docked. Thirty-one checks are now in tests/test_fit.py, including the comparison with Extrutim's clip.

Open

Smaller items

Label sizes still vary by material (auto-fit). The thin 0.4 mm lip edge at the rim may print ragged. Sticker thickness is unmeasured, and the 0.6 mm pocket assumes about 0.4 mm. Two-colour import into Snapmaker Orca hasn't been tried.

What the checks do and don't show

Passing: watertight parts that add up to the dock; no chip overlaps its dock; a chip lowered from 30 mm seats without touching; chips 1.8 mm off-centre, 0.4 mm forward or 0.6 mm back drop into the funnel; a flipped chip stops on the key post; the back plate covers the whole sticker; the sticker pocket is sealed; labels stay inside the edge; the hook finger stands at least 2 mm proud of the lower pocket. The clip matches Extrutim's original within 0.1 mm wherever it was sampled. All but that last check use numbers from the same script that builds the parts. They show the files agree with themselves, not that anything fits a printer or a real sticker.
